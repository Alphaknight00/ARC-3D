Capabilities

Everything you need to design

A complete toolkit for residential and commercial architectural workflows.

Wall System

Solid, cavity, and stud walls with automatic mitered corners, snapping, and centerline geometry.

Doors & Windows

Insert openings into walls with automatic wall cutting, hinge sides, and configurable dimensions.

Roof Modeling

Hip, gable, and flat roof types with face splitting, tapering, and structural framing.

Staircases

Straight, U-shaped, and spiral stairs with stringers, railings, landings, and material presets.

Multi-Story Levels

Ground, first, second floors and basement with custom elevations and per-level visibility.

Project Persistence

Auto-save every 5 minutes with IndexedDB storage, project folders, and JSON export.

Import & Export

DXF and IFC import, plus PDF and image export for sharing drawings with clients and contractors.

Real-Time Rendering

Babylon.js 6.0 powered 3D viewport with shadows, materials, skybox, and orbit controls.

Workflow

From sketch to model in minutes

A streamlined four-step process built for speed.

STEP 01
Draft in 2D
Lay out walls, doors, and windows on the orthographic grid with snapping and dimensioning.
STEP 02
Add Levels
Stack floors, place staircases, and configure elevations for multi-story buildings.
STEP 03
Visualize in 3D
Press 3 to enter perspective view with shadows, materials, and orbit controls.
STEP 04
Export & Share
Save as a project, export to PDF, or hand off DXF files to engineering teams.
